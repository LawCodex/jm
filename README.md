# Jamaica — Laws of Jamaica (LawCodex)

Revised (consolidated) statutes of **Jamaica** as plain Markdown, converted from the official PDFs of the
**Laws of Jamaica** website of the Ministry of Justice (Law Reform Department / Legal Reform),
<https://laws.moj.gov.jm> → Library → *Revised Laws of Jamaica*.

Retrieved: 2026-10-13.

## Contents

| Directory | What | Files |
|---|---|---|
| `constitution/` | The Jamaica (Constitution) Order in Council 1962 (the Constitution of Jamaica is its Second Schedule) | 1 |
| `chapters/` | Revised statutes (`<slug>.md`, from the site's statute slug without the leading "the-") | 600 |

Total: **601 of the 669 statutes** listed under *Revised Laws of Jamaica*. The site does not expose chapter
numbers, so file names are title slugs. Front matter: `title`, `identifier` (Act number, when given),
`type`, `date` (operational date), `last_amended` (the site's "Last Amendment" date), `long_title`,
`legal_areas`, `source` (statute page), `source_pdf`, `text_source`, `pages`, `retrieved`.

## Important caveat — OCR text

Most PDFs on the site (584 of the converted files) are page scans carrying an **embedded OCR text layer
produced by the publisher** (Adobe Acrobat Paper Capture). That layer is used as-is — LawCodex performed
no OCR — so the Markdown contains recognition errors (misread letters, stray characters, page-authority
notes such as "[The inclusion of this page is authorized by L.N. …]" are removed where recognisable).
Such files are marked in `text_source`. Mean dictionary-word ratio of the converted text is
~0.94 (minimum 0.79). Always check against the official PDF before relying on a passage.

Marginal notes are rendered as *italic* lines before the paragraph they sit beside; section numbers in
**bold**; tables are flattened to text.

## Source and licence

Official texts of the Government of Jamaica published by the Ministry of Justice. The site has no
robots.txt and no published terms of use. This is an unofficial conversion; the official PDFs remain
authoritative. Note: the server does not send its intermediate TLS certificate; the fetcher adds
DigiCert's published intermediate ("DigiCert Global G2 TLS RSA SHA256 2020 CA1") to the CA bundle
instead of disabling verification.

## Regenerate

```
python3 scripts/jm_fetch.py statutes [K N]   # DataTables JSON list, statute pages and PDFs -> ../_raw/jm (optional K-of-N sharding)
python3 scripts/jm_convert.py                # pdftotext -bbox-layout -> Markdown (scripts/jm_pdfmd.py)
```

Requires poppler (`pdftotext`, `pdfinfo`) and `scripts/requirements.txt`. PDFs are verified (header,
`%%EOF`, `pdfinfo`, size) before conversion; downloads are streamed with HTTP Range resume.
