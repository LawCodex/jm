Category of CARICOM Nationals Exempt f om Work Permit Requirement

CARICOM Nationals who, in accordance with the Caribbean Community (Establishment, Services, Capital and Movement of Community Nationals) 'Act, are already in, or are coming to, Jamaica to exercise rights of establishment, provide services or move capital.
