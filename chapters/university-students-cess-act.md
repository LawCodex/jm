**3.** (1) The House of Representatives may from time to time by resolution impose a cess to be paid by sponsored students and may revoke, reduce, increase or alter any cess and may provide for exemptions therefrom.

**4.** The Minister may, upon application made by a person liable to pay cess, waive, remit or refund, in whole or in part, any such cess if, in the circumstances of the case, he deems it expedient to do so; and any such waiver, remission or refund may be subject to such special conditions as the Minister may see fit to impose.

**5.** The Commissioner shall refund any money which he is satisfied has been overpaid as cess at any time within two years after such overpayment has been made.

**7.** The cess imposed by this Act in respect of each academic year shall be paid in full upon registration of the sponsored student at the University for that year:

Provided that the Commissioner may accept, on such terms and conditions as he thinks fit, an assurance that payment will be made within a period not exceeding three months after registration.

**8.** (1) The Students' Loan Bureau shall make such returns to the Commissioner at such times and shall keep such records in such form and substance as the Commis-

sioner may from time to time require or as may be prescribed.

(2) The Commissioner may enlarge the time for making any return before or after the time for making it.

**9.** (1) Where there is any doubt as to thc amount of cess payable by any sponsored student the Commissioner, after consultation with the University, shall make an assessment of the cess to be paid by that student and shall notify the student accordingly.

(2) An assessment, subject to being varied or vacated on an objection and subject to a reassessment, shall be deemed to be valid and binding notwithstanding any error, defect or omission therein or in any proceeding under this Act relating thereto.

**10.** (1) Where a sponsorsd student objects to an assessment made under section 9, he may, within ten days from the date of receipt of the notice of assessment, serve on the Commissioner a notice of objection in duplicate in a form approved by the Commissioner, setting out the reasons for the objection and all relevant facts.
