# The Law Reform (Commorientes) Act

THE LAW REFORM (COMMORIENTES) A m '

*Act 50of 1968.*

[27th December, 1968.1

*shorttitle.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Law Reform (Com-morientes) Act.

*Presumption of survivor-*

**2.** In all cases where, after the 27th December, 1968, two or more persons have died in circumstances rendering it uncertain which of them survived the other or others, such deaths shall for all purposes affecting title to property, be presumed to have occurred in order of seniority and accordingly the younger shall be deemed to have survived the elder.
