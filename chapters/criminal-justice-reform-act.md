**13.** (1) Where a person who has attained the age of eighteen years is convicted of any offence (other than murder) punishable with imprisonment, the court may, instead of dealing with him in any other way, make an order (hereinafter called a curfew order) requiring the offender to remain at a place specified in the order for a period of time so specified.

(2) A curfew order may specify different places or different periods for different days, being periods-

(a) not exceeding six months from the date of the order; or

(b) amounting to not more than twelve hours in any one day.
