# The Kingdom of Lesotho Act

THE KINGDOM OF LESOTHO ACT Ad 36 of 1967.

[4rh October, 1966.1

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Kingdom of Lesotho Shorttitle. Act.

**2.** In this Act-lnterprela-tion.

“commencement of this Act” means 4th October, 1966;

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for external affairs;

“Parliament” means the Parliament of Jamaica.

3.41) On and after the commencement of this Act, all Operation of existing law, that is to say all law which, whether being a rule of law or a provision of an Act of Parliament or of law. any other enactment or instrument whatsoever, is in force on the date of such commencement or has been passed or made before that date and comes into force thereafter, shall, unless and until provision to the contrary is made by Parliament or some other authority have power in that behalf, and subject to the following provisions of this section, have the same operation in relation to Lesotho, and persons and things belonging to or connected with Lesotho, as it would have apart from this subsection if on that date Basutoland had been renamed Lesotho, but there had beefi no change in its status.

(2) The Minister may, by order, make such adapta-tions in any enactment passed before or after the commencement of this Act, or in any instrument having effect under any such enactment, as appear to him necessary or

expedient in consequence of the change in the status of Basutoland on that date; and any such order may, though made after the cornmencement of this Act, be made so as to have effect from the date of such commencement.

(3) Every order made under subsection (2) shall be subject to negative resolution.
