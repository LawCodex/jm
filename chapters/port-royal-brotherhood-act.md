Preliminary

*short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Port Royal Brotherhood Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act-

“the Board” means the Board of Directors of the Brotherhood, to be established in accordance with this Act;

“the Brotherhood” means the Port Royal Brotherhood to be established in accordance with this Act;

“the Chairman” means the Chairman of the Brotherhood and includes any person appointed in accordance with subsection (2) of section 5 to act as Chairman;

“the Committee” means the Assessment Committee to be constituted under subsection (1) of section 16;

“the relevant area” has the meaning assigned to it by subsection (1) of section 15;

“the Secretary” means the Secretary of the Brotherhood.
