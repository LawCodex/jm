### THE LEGITIMATION ACT

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Legitimation Act.

**2.** Any child born before the marriage of his or her parents whose parents have intermarried or shall hereafter intermarry shall be deemed on the marriage of such parents to have been legitimated as from the date of such marriage and shall be entitled to all the rights of a child born in wedlock.
