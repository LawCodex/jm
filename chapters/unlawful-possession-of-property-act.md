# The Unlawful Possession of Property Act

### THE UNLAWFUL POSSESSION OF PROPERTY ACT '

**2.** Interpretation.

**3.** Selection of authorized persons.

**4.** Remuneration of authorized persons.

**5.** Arrest and trial of suspected persons.

**6.** Power of constable or aut.horized person on reasonable cause for belief.

**7.** Powers of authorized person extend only to agricultural produce.

**8.** Search warrant.

**9.** Power to board vessel, etc.

**10.** Trial of person brought before Resident Magistrate under sections 8 and 9.

**11.** Tracing possession.

**12.** Power of constables and authorized persons to summon assistance.

**13.** Penalty for assaulting, etc., constables, authorized persons and their assistants.

**14.** Power to vary Schedule.
