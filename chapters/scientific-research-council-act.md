Preliminary

*short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Scientific Research Council Act.

*Interprets-tion.*

**2.** In this Act unless the context otherwise requires-

"chairman" means the chairman of the Council and includes any person appointed to act as chairman of the Council;

"Council" means the Scientific Research Council established in accordance with the provisions of this Act;

"invention" includes any new process or new, technique, whether or not a patent could be granted in relation thereto;

"member" means a member of the Council and includes the chairman.

Miscellaneous

*Regulations.*

**14.** The Council may with the approval of the Minister make regulations-

(a) determining generally the conditions of service of officers and servants of the Council;

(b) relating in particular, but without prejudice to the generality of the provisions of paragraphs (a) and (c), to-

(i) the grant of pensions, gratuities and other benefits to such officers and servants and their dependants, and the grant of gratuities and other benefits to the dependants or estates of deceased officers and servants of the Council;

(ii) the establishment and maintenance of sick funds, superannuation funds and provident funds, the contributions payable thereto and the benefits receivable therefrom;

(c) generally for the better carrying out of the purposes of this Act.
