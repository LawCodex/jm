*Objects of ! , Act.*

**3.** The objects of this Act are to-

( a ) establish a regulatory scheme for the conduct of casino gaming in Jamaica;

( b ) encourage the establishment of a number of substantial integrated resort developments by licensing the carrying on of casino gaming as part of a mix of various tourism amenities and facilities available to guests of hotels in such developments;

(c) ensure that casino gaming is con'dqcted in a fair and open manner;

(d) ensure that casino gaming is regulated efficiently and effectively;

( e ) prevent casino ,gaming from being a source of crime or disorder or being used to support crime; and

V) protect children and other vulnerable persons Erom exploitation due to casino gaming.
