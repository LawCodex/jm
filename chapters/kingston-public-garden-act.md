*Powers of a keeper in uniform.*

**6.** Any keeper in uniform and any person whom he may call to his assistance, may take into custody, without a warrant, any offender who, in the public garden, and within the view of such keeper, acts in contravention of any of the said regulations :

Provided that the name and residence of such offender are unknown to and cannot be ascertained by such keeper. If any such offender, when required by any keeper or by any constable to give his name and address gives a false name or false address, he shall on summary conviction be liable to a penalty not exceeding ten dollars.
