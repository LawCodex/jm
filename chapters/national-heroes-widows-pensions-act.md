(3) A pension payable pursuant to subsection (1) shall be payable-

(a) in the case of a person who was a widow of a National Hero on the 1st day of April, 1972, with effect from that date;

(b) in the case of a widow of a person upon whom the honour of National Hero is conferred posthumously after the 1st day of April, 1972, with effect from the date on which the honour is so conferred;

(c) in any other case, with effect from the day immediately following the date of the National Hero's death.

(4) Where pension is payable under subsection (I) to a widow who, apart from the provision of this section, is en-titled to pension in respect of her deceased husband's public service, she shall be paid at her election either the pension under subsection (1) or the pension in respect of her deceased husband's public service but not both.

(5) An election under subsection (4) shall be in Writing addressed to the Minister responsible for finance and shall be irrevocable.

(6) Nothing in subsection (4) shall prevent payment to a widow of any pension to which she is entitled in respect of her own public service.
