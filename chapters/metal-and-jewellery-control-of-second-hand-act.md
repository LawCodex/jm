*short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Metal and Jewellery (Control of Second-hand) Act.

*Interpretation-*

**2.** In this Act “jewellery” includes precious and semiprecious stones and all articles of personal use or adornment composed wholly or partly of gold, silver, platinum, or gold plate or silver plate.

**3.** No person shall deal in any second-hand metal or any second-hand jewellery except in accordance with a licence under this Act.

*Mode of application for licence.*

**4.** (1) Every person who desires to be licensed to deal in second-hand metal or in second-hand jewellery shall make application in the prescribed form to the Resident Magistrate for the parish in which he desires so to deal.

(2) Every application under subsection (1) shall specify the premises upon which the applicant desires to deal ‘in second-hand metal or second-hand jewellery and shall be accompanied by a fee of one dollar.

(3) Every person who intends to apply for a licence under this Act shall give not less than seven days’ notice of his intention so to do to the Superintendent of Police in charge of the parish in which are situated the premises upon which he desires to deal in second-hand metal or secondhand jewellery.

*Transfer of licence.*

**7.** The Resident Magistrate for any parish may, on the application of any person licensed under section 5 to deal in second-hand metal or second-hand jewellery upon any premises situated in that parish, order that such licence be transferred to any other premises situated in that parish, and thereupon all the provisions of this Act shall apply to the premises specified in such order as if those premises had been the premises specified in an application under section 4.

**11.** No licensee shall dispose of, disfigure or in any way change the form or the shape of any second-hand metal or second-hand jewellery within fifteen days of acquiring such second-hand metal or second-hand jewellery except with the written permission of a Superintendent of Police.

*Licensee to notify police of specified articles known to be stolen, etc.*

**12.** Every licensee who, after notice has been given to him by any constable or by advertisement in the Gazette that any specified articles have been stolen or fraudulently obtained, fails to give information to the police that articles of the like description were offered to him or were or are in his possession, shall be guilty of an offence against this Act.

*Search warrant.*

*Schedule.*

**13.** (1) Any Justice, upon being satisfied by evidence on oath that there is reasonable cause to believe that any second-hand metal or second-hand jewellery which has been stolen or otherwise unlawfully obtained is upon any premises belonging to or occupied by any licensee, may grant a warrant in the form in the Schedule authorizing any constable with such assistance as may be necessary to enter any premises specified in the warrant and to take any second-hand metal or second-hand jewellery found therein before the Clerk of the Courts for the parish.

(2) Where any person who is upon any premises at the time when a warrant under subsection (1) is in the course of being executed is suspected of concealing about his person any second-hand metal or second-hand jewellery, he may be searched and any second-hand metal or secondhand jewellery found upon him shall be taken before the Clerk of the Courts for the parish.

(3) Where pursuant to the provisions of subsection

(1) or subsection (2) any second-hand metal or second-hand jewellery is taken before a Clerk of the Courts, he shall issue a summons requiring the licensee of the premises upon which such second-hand metal or second-hand jewel-lery was found or the person upon whom such second-hand metal or second-hand jewellery was found (as the case may be) to appear before the Resident Magistrate at a time and place specified in the summons to account for his possession of such second-hand metal or second-hand jewellery.

(4) Every licensee or person upon whom any secondhand metal or second-hand jewellery is found who fails to satisfy any Resident Magistrate that his possession of such second-hand metal or second-hand jewellery was lawfully obtained shall be guilty of an offence against this Act.

*Penaltyfor offences.*

**14.** Every person who acts in contravention of any of the provisions of this Act for which no special punishment is

provided by this Act, shall be guilty of an offence against this Act and shall upon summary conviction before a Resident Magistrate be liable to a fine not exceeding two hundred dollars or to imprisonment for any term not exceeding twelve months or to both such fine and such imprisonment.

*Cancellation of licence.*

**15.** When any person licensed under this Act is convicted of an offence against this Act, the Court may, if it sees fit, cancel the licence of such person.

*Power to make regulations.*

**16.** The Minister may make regulations prescribing-

(a) the form in which application shall be made under section 4;

(b) the form of licence under section 5; and

(c) the books to be kept under this Act and the particulars to be entered therein.
