# The Mandeville Parade Act

*Cap. 235.*

### MANDEVILLE PARADE ACT

[2Ist May, I890.1

*Shorttitle.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Mandeville Parade Act.

*Powerto make regulations as Elze~lle Parade.*

**2.** The Parish Council of the parish of Manchester may make regulations for the proper management and preservation of the Mandeville Parade, and may from time to time alter, add to or rescind, any such regulations :

Provided always that no such regulations, or any alteration or amendment thereof, shall be of any force or effect until fourteen days after the same shall have been approved by the Minister and published in the Gazette.

*Breachof regulations, penalty theref or.*

**3.** Any person doing any act in contravention of any regulation made by the Parish Council under this Act, and approved as aforesaid, shall, on summary conviction be liable to a penalty not exceeding ten dollars, or in default to imprisonment with or without hard labour for a term not exceeding one month.

*Powerof arrest.*

**4.** Any constable, or other person specially appointed in writing by the Chairman of the Parish Council, may lawfully arrest without warrant any person wilfully and after due warning contravening any regulation of the Council which has been approved as aforesaid.

*Powers,etc.,*

*carry regulations. out*

**5.** Every person appointed in writing by the Chairman to carry out the regulations of the Parish Council shall, for the purposes of this Act, have the powers and privileges of a constable of the Jamaica Constabulary.

*Postingup of regulations.*

**6.** When rules and regulations have been approved by the Minister, the Parish Council aforesaid shall cause copies thereof to be painted on boards, and posted and kept posted up on two conspicuous places on or near the Parade.
