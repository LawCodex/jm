*Functions Of Trust.*

**4.** (1) The functions of the Trust shall be-

(a) to promote the preservation of national monuments and anything designated as protected national heritage for the benefit of the Island;

(b) to conduct such research as it thinks necessary or desirable for the purposes of the performance of its functions under this Act;

(c) to carry out such development as it considers necessary for the preservation of any national monument or anything designated as protected national heritage;

(d) to record any precious objects or works of art to be preserved and to identify and record any species of botanical or animal life to be protected.
